Reading the specs

How hard the ink pulls back at the roller. Low tack for soft, uncoated cotton; high tack for coated stock that will not absorb.

The largest particle left after the second pass. Under 8 µm holds a 6 pt serif without filling in.

Graded on the Blue Wool scale. Grade I holds for decades indoors; grade II will shift in a sunlit frame.
