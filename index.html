Milled in 4 kg batches

Granite triple-roll, closing to 6 µm. Stone keeps the linseed cool, so the ink does not skin early.

Rested 14 days

Sealed in a 14 °C cellar while the tack settles. Ink sold sooner prints thin and keeps shifting in the tin.

Proofed on press

Every batch is pulled on rag, coated and newsprint. The proof is stapled to the batch sheet and kept ten years.

Ink is a recipe, not a colour

A pigment behaves differently in raw linseed than it does in a boiled or rubber-modified base. Two inks can match on a swatch and print nothing alike — one lays down flat on soft cotton, the other skids across coated stock and fills in the counters.

So we sell inks by press and by paper, not by colour name alone. Tell us what you print on and we will tell you which base to take.
